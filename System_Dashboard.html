09 / OPERATIONS NOTES

Operations notes & methodology

Open methodology and limitations

Strategic placement

Monitor approximately 50 critical locations chosen from about 2,000 drains using drainage maps, historical flood data and engineering assessment. Prioritise retention pond inlets and outlets, historical flashpoints, tide gates and main arterial culverts. The 11 nodes shown here are representative, not exact surveyed geometry.

Dry-weather monitoring

Unexpected accumulation or flow can indicate a fault. Consider wastewater discharge and other causes before diagnosing a blockage.

Communications and freshness

ESP32 nodes can check anomalies locally. LoRa carries compact readings and alert messages to an internet-connected gateway; remote or WhatsApp delivery needs internet. Camera images need Wi-Fi or another higher-bandwidth connection. Offline values must be labelled stale.

Operational value

Target fewer manual inspections, faster response, reduced avoidable flood damage, lower maintenance costs, and safer, more sustainable townships. Thresholds, classifications and predicted outcomes need site calibration and field validation.
